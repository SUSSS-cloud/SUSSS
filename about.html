Welcome to SUSSS

Salale University Special Secondary School (SUSSS) unites the region's brightest students, offering top-quality education in sciences and fostering excellence, discipline, leadership, and innovation. Under Director Gudeta Adugna, SUSSS shapes future leaders ready for higher education and national impact.

Our Mission

Salale University Special Secondary School is committed to providing quality education, developing skills, and nurturing responsible citizens who will contribute to the advancement of society and the nation.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution that produces academically excellent and morally upright students who will become leaders in their respective fields and drive positive change in Ethiopia and beyond.

Our Core Values

  • Excellence: We strive for the highest standards in all aspects of education and personal development.
  • Discipline: We maintain a structured environment that promotes focus, responsibility, and self-control.
  • Leadership: We cultivate leadership skills in our students, preparing them to take on important roles in society.
  • Innovation: We encourage creative thinking and innovative approaches to problem-solving.
  • Integrity: We uphold honesty, ethics, and moral principles in all our endeavors.

School Leadership

Under the visionary leadership of Director Gudeta Adugna, SUSSS has established itself as a premier institution for secondary education in the region. Director Adugna brings years of educational experience and a deep commitment to student success, ensuring that every student receives the support and opportunities they need to excel.

Our Students

SUSSS brings together the brightest and most talented students from across the region. Our students are selected based on their academic merit and potential, and they represent the future leaders, scientists, engineers, doctors, and innovators of Ethiopia.

Facilities & Resources

Our school is equipped with modern facilities and resources to support comprehensive education in sciences and other disciplines. We provide:

  • Well-equipped science laboratories
  • Modern classrooms with learning technologies
  • Comprehensive library resources
  • Sports and recreational facilities
  • Comfortable boarding facilities for students

Preparing for the Future

At SUSSS, we don't just prepare students for exams – we prepare them for life. Our comprehensive curriculum, combined with extracurricular activities and leadership development programs, ensures that our graduates are well-equipped for higher education and ready to make meaningful contributions to national development.
