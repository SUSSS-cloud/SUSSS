Academic Programs

SUSSS offers a comprehensive curriculum with a strong emphasis on sciences, designed to prepare students for success in higher education and beyond.

Science Focus Areas

Mathematics

Advanced mathematics courses including algebra, geometry, calculus, and statistics

Physics

Comprehensive physics program covering mechanics, electricity, magnetism, and modern physics

Chemistry

In-depth chemistry studies including organic, inorganic, and physical chemistry

Biology

Extensive biology curriculum covering molecular biology, genetics, ecology, and anatomy

Additional Subjects

  • English Language and Literature
  • Amharic Language
  • History and Social Studies
  • Geography
  • Information Technology
  • Physical Education

Academic Excellence

Our students consistently achieve outstanding results in national examinations and university entrance exams. We provide:

  • Small class sizes for personalized attention
  • Experienced and dedicated teachers
  • Modern laboratories and learning facilities
  • Regular assessments and progress monitoring
  • Additional tutoring and support programs
  • Preparation for national and international competitions

Student Achievement

Our students have gone on to attend prestigious universities both nationally and internationally, pursuing careers in medicine, engineering, research, and various other fields.
