About Our School

Salale University Special Secondary School (SUSSS) is a premier educational institution dedicated to providing exceptional education to the brightest students in the region. Our school is committed to excellence in sciences, fostering discipline, developing leadership qualities, and promoting innovation.

Our Mission

To unite the region's brightest students and provide them with top-quality education in sciences, preparing them for higher education and enabling them to make meaningful contributions to national development.

Our Vision

To be recognized as a leading special secondary school that shapes future leaders through excellence in education, discipline, and innovation.

Our Values

  • Excellence: We strive for the highest standards in all academic and extracurricular endeavors
  • Discipline: We promote self-discipline and responsibility in all aspects of school life
  • Leadership: We nurture leadership qualities in every student
  • Innovation: We encourage creative thinking and problem-solving
  • Integrity: We uphold honesty and ethical behavior in all our actions

History

SUSSS was established to serve the brightest students in the Salale region, providing them with specialized education that prepares them for success in higher education and beyond. Under the leadership of Director Gudeta Adugna, the school has maintained its commitment to academic excellence and character development.
